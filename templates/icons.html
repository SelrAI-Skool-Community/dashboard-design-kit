How to use one

In markup: Icons.svg('search') or Icons.solid('wallet','lg'). Sizes are sm 15px, default 18px, lg 22px, set by the .icon class in base.css.

Icons inherit currentColor, so they follow the text colour they sit next to. Never hard-set a fill on one.

Solid is the same geometry with a soft fill, not a second hand-drawn set. One source of truth means the two styles cannot drift apart.
