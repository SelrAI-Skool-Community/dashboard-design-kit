How to use one

Every chart is one call into charts.js. Example: Charts.bars([12,28,19,44,22,31], {highlight:3, tip:'$27,632', hatch:true, xLabels:[…]}).

Colours are always token variables (--c1 to --c5, --c-negative), so light and dark come free and a rebrand is one file.

The engine is deterministic. No random data, so a render is reproducible and a screenshot diff means something.
